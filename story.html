Rooted in Grapevine

The good finds
keep coming.

Kiss It Good Buy opened in 2002 and moved to 201 East Franklin Street in 2017. The gallery brings furniture, art, lighting, and accessories together in historic downtown Grapevine.

A good find should feel right in your room and in your budget. The gallery considers age, condition, original price, and market value when pricing pieces—with care for both the person consigning and the person taking it home.
