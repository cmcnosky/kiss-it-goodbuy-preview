Clear expectations, from the start

Know the arrangement.

Based on the store’s published consignment information. Confirm the current agreement with the gallery.

How long is the consignment period?

The published term is 90 days. The gallery describes a possible extension at its discretion or donation of unsold items with a donation slip. Discuss the disposition of your piece before signing the agreement.

How are prices and proceeds handled?

The published proceeds split is 50/50. The store describes scheduled markdowns of 10–20% every 30 days, capped at 50% within the contract period. Confirm your item’s starting price and schedule in the agreement.

What happens after my piece sells?

The published policy says proceeds become payable 30 days after a sale. Checks are processed on the 20th by request and available for pickup on or after the 21st. Contact the gallery to confirm your balance and request payment.

Can I bring pieces in without an appointment?

Furniture must be approved first. The store schedules drop-offs on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays. For more than five accessories, prepare an item list.

Can the gallery help with transport?

The gallery can recommend third-party movers. Confirm costs and arrangements directly before booking.
