Before you make the trip

A few useful answers.

Have a question about a specific piece? Keep its listing handy when you call so the gallery can help you find the right details.

817-481-9754
Can I buy online?

Yes. Open a selected piece and follow “Check availability & buy” to the store’s existing online shop. Check its current price, availability, condition, and pickup details before purchasing.

Do you ship furniture?

The current shipping policy specifies local pickup. The store can recommend local movers; out-of-area buyers are directed to arrange their own transport. Read the current transport policy ↗.

Can I return a purchase?

Ask the gallery before buying. The published refund policy describes refunds as negotiated in store, issued by check, with a 3.3% transaction fee deducted. It does not specify all eligibility conditions. Read the current refund policy ↗.

Can I bring in something to consign?

Start with photos and details by email. Furniture must be approved before drop-off, and appointments are required. Prepare a consignment inquiry.

Is every pictured piece still available?

This design preview uses a dated catalog selection and archival social imagery. Use the live listing or call the gallery to confirm a particular piece before making a trip.
